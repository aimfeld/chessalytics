## Decisions Made
- Landed 21 total positions (one above the plan's implied ~20 floor) by adding one extra middlegame and one extra endgame position beyond the stated per-category minimums, as buffer margin against the one-third early-position cap and the category-count acceptance criteria.
- Bumped the fullmove counters on all 5 non-built-in synthetic endgame FENs from the conventional study-diagram value of 1 into the 30s-40s range. Left at 1, the widened set's "early" count (fullmove_number<=4) would have been exactly 7/21 — passing the `3*early<=len(wide)` check only because it lands exactly at the boundary (21<=21), which is a coincidence of the built-in endgame FEN's own fullmove counter (also pinned at 1, unmodifiable since it's copied verbatim) plus the 5 new ones. Bumping the 5 new ones to a later fullmove number drops early count to 2 and removes the reliance on an unintended boundary coincidence, since these positions represent hypothetical middle-of-a-game endgame studies, not literal move-1 positions.
- Filled the 400-node subset's two non-built-in slots with the Fried Liver Attack (sharp/tactical) and the knight-and-pawns ending (endgame), diversifying against the built-in's own rook/pawn ending rather than duplicating its piece-type profile.
- Generated all opening/middlegame/sharp FENs by replaying named theoretical SAN sequences through python-chess rather than hand-typing FEN strings, so legality is machine-verified at authoring time rather than relying on manual transcription accuracy.

## Deviations from Plan

None - plan executed exactly as written. Two internal correction cycles occurred during authoring (not deviations from the plan, but bugs caught before commit): a hand-built knight endgame FEN and a hand-built bishop endgame FEN both initially placed the minor piece on a square that gave an illegal OPPOSITE_CHECK position (the piece attacked the opposing king while it was that king's opponent's move) — both were caught by python-chess's `is_valid()` during authoring, before either file was written to disk, and fixed by relocating the piece off the attacking square.
