<objective>
Make every Node-side grading path speak the same `go` as the shipped browser, and give the
decision harness the two capabilities LADDER-01 and LADDER-05 actually need but that it does not
have today: a real ladder mode, and a direct measurement of D-07's warm-hash question.

Two of these are landmines rather than features. `scripts/lib/stockfish-pool.mjs`'s `grade`
closure declares two parameters, and JavaScript silently discards extra arguments passed to a
function with fewer declared parameters — so the moment Plan 01's `dispatchExpansion` starts
passing a 4th depth argument, the REAL calibration pool that Phase 199's recalibration sweep runs
against would grade every node at one flat depth while the browser ran the ladder, and nothing in
the sweep's own output would show it. And `engine-grading-depth-ab.mjs`'s `gradeAtDepth` closes
over ONE depth for an entire search pass, so it structurally cannot produce LADDER-05's
ladder-vs-flat-14 comparison.

Purpose: LADDER-04's harness/browser parity, plus the harness capability LADDER-01 and LADDER-05
consume.
Output: one shared `go` builder across every call site, a depth-forwarding calibration pool, a
ladder mode, and a D-07 hash probe.
</objective>
