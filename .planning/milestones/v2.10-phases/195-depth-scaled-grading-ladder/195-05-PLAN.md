<objective>
Run the widened measurement LADDER-01 requires and derive, mechanically, the inputs Plan 06's
rung commit consumes — then stop and show the operator, before anything is committed into the
shipped engine.

The stop is deliberate. Phase 199 runs one combined recalibration sweep against whatever ladder
ships from this phase, so the rungs are costly to change afterwards: reversing them later
invalidates the calibration they were measured through. The operator sees the frontier, the D-07
finding, and the three mechanically-derived candidates before the multi-hour Stage B runs begin.

Purpose: LADDER-01's committed data.
Output: two committed TSVs, a Stage A findings document, and an operator decision.
</objective>

  into a boundary/precision/concurrency shape, and the planner did not invent one. The planner's
  assumption is that LADDER-01's failure mode is procedural rather than numeric — the rungs get

  requirement names. Both are addressed by ordering (the accept rule and the position set land in

  for example a position whose depth ordering is non-monotone in a way the accept rule's
  aggregate statistics hide — it is NOT covered by this plan and must be raised at the checkpoint
  rather than assumed absent.
