<objective>
Give the Node harness the WDL surface the app now ships, then measure the handoff depth and put the
choice in front of the developer.

Sequencing is load-bearing: `scripts/lib/node-engine-providers.mjs` and
`scripts/lib/calibration-providers.mjs` carry ZERO WDL code today (grep-confirmed:
no `wdl`/`Wdl`/`WDL` match in either). If any LEAF-02/04/07 evidence run happens before this
plumbing lands, it measures a policy-only mirror of the engine rather than shipped behaviour, and
the resulting number is worthless. Plumb first, measure second.

The measurement's baseline is also load-bearing. Premise correction **P-02**: SEED-126's advertised
2-5x was measured against flat depth 14, and Phase 195's shipped `GRADING_DEPTH_LADDER = [14,14,14]`
/ `GRADING_DEPTH_FLOOR = 10` already banked 1.37x at 50 nodes and 2.00x at 400 nodes of it. What
this phase can still buy is the difference against the POST-ladder engine, and it will look far
smaller than the seed implied. That is an honest sequencing finding, not a failure of either phase,
and the report must say so out loud.

Purpose: LEAF-02 — choose the handoff depth from measurement, stated explicitly against the Phase
195 ladder.
Output: harness WDL plumbing, a committed TSV per arm under `reports/data/`, a narrated
`reports/leaf-wdl/report.md`, and a developer decision on the depth (including an explicit early
exit).
</objective>
