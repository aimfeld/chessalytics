D-15 step 6 — the in-place rewrite of `mctsSearch.ts`'s dispatch loop.

The mechanical delta is narrower than "replace the loop". `isPending` already clears at commit,
visits already increment at apply time, and `selectChild`'s inputs are already a pure function of

and the node-budget guard moves from a round-scoped proxy to the persistent in-flight counter.

revert is `git revert`.

  <action>
Rewrite the dispatch loop in place. No second runner behind a constant, no retained round loop —
`npm run knip` would flag an unreferenced runner anyway, and the operator's post-197 standard is to
strip rather than bloat.

Replace the round-scoped expansion array with three persistent pieces of loop state: an in-flight
counter incremented at dispatch and decremented at commit; a dispatch sequence and a commit
sequence; and a fixed-size commit ring buffer of length `budget.concurrency` indexed by dispatch
sequence modulo that length. Under `noUncheckedIndexedAccess` every read from that array is
possibly-undefined — narrow with a local binding and an explicit check, never with a non-null
assertion at the drain condition and never with a suppression comment.

Keep the inner selection loop's body essentially verbatim, including the freshly-discovered-dead-end
branch with its single visit bump and closure propagation, the depth-ceiling flag, and the pending
marker set at exactly the same point on exactly the same statement. What changes is only the loop's
stopping condition and what happens after: instead of collecting a round's worth and then awaiting
them together, dispatch each expansion immediately — call `dispatchExpansion`, do not await it here,
attach exactly ONE settlement handler to its promise at that moment, and continue selecting while a
slot is free and the node budget allows.

The settlement handler writes the resolved expansion into the ring slot for its own dispatch
sequence and pings a single shared wake signal. Attach a rejection path in the same wiring that
writes a degenerate empty-candidate result instead, so a rejecting provider degrades exactly the way
a zero-candidate provider already does rather than escaping as an unhandled rejection. Never re-race
a mutating set of promises: each dispatched promise gets one handler, once, at dispatch time.

The wake signal is a single shared promise replaced on every wake. It must carry no identity — only
the fact that something settled. Which commit becomes visible is decided solely by the commit
sequence, which is what keeps arrival order out of selection.

Replace the round's apply loop with a drain that consumes the ring buffer strictly in commit-sequence
order while the next slot is populated: take the entry, clear the slot, decrement in flight,
increment the commit sequence, then run the existing apply body byte-for-byte — apply the expansion,
skip the committed-node increment for a degenerate zero-candidate result, flag budget exhaustion at
the ceiling, evaluate the stop rule, emit the snapshot, and break out on early stop. That body is
what preserves the stop rule firing once per applied expansion in a strictly ordered sequence.

Handle the two null cases explicitly. A null selection with work in flight awaits the wake signal and
retries from the top. A null selection with nothing in flight is the tree-fully-searched case and
breaks with today's semantics — not budget exhaustion by itself.

Move the node-budget guard from the round-scoped proxy to committed nodes plus in flight against
`budget.maxNodes`. Preserve the rare under-dispatch when a dispatched expansion turns out degenerate;
do not fix that asymmetry here, because doing so would be an unattributed strength change riding
along with the rewrite.

On early stop or abort, stop dispatching and return. Do not drain, do not await outstanding promises,
do not add a cleanup step that waits for them — an outstanding promise resolving into a slot nobody
reads is garbage-collected normally, and that is the design.

Do not touch `dispatchExpansion`'s body. Do not touch `treeCommon.ts`, `select.ts`,
`fallbackExpectimax.ts`, or `guardrail.ts`. If any new tunable is genuinely needed, make it a named
exported constant with a doc comment tracing it to a committed measurement or a named prior decision;
the ring length is `budget.concurrency` itself and needs no constant.

If the reviewed mechanism proves unimplementable as written, stop and raise it as a checkpoint rather
than improvising a variant — most of all, do not narrow toward the prefetch-only shape, which was
explicitly rejected on 2026-07-30.
