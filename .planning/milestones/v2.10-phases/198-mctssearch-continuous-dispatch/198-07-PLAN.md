<objective>
DISPATCH-09, -10 and -11 — activate the dormant priority queue honestly, and prove the two
contracts the rewrite must not have broken.

SEED-127's premise for DISPATCH-09 does not follow, and the requirement has to be met on honest
terms (D-09). One expansion issues exactly one batched grade, so a window of width `c` never
exceeds `c` in-flight grades; with the analysis board at pool-size concurrency and the bot pinned
at 4 against a 4-process pool, the pending array still stays empty in production and
`dequeueHighestPriority` still goes unreached. Continuous dispatch alone does not change that.
So: wire the real values the `workerPool.ts` header has specified since Phase 154, prove
reachability with a unit test at concurrency above pool size, and do NOT raise shipped concurrency
to manufacture it — that is D-10's explicit don't-touch, and the deferred item that would make the
queue genuinely live has its own future unit.

There is one plan-time tension worth naming: D-09 needs priority and depth to reach
`WorkerPool.grade`, and the only caller is `dispatchExpansion`, whose body D-13 requires to stay
byte-unchanged. The resolution is a per-dispatch provider shim built by the loop — the values are
computed where the loop already holds the path, and `dispatchExpansion` sees an ordinary providers
object. If that proves unworkable, the changed function is a checkpoint, not a silent accept.

Purpose: meet DISPATCH-09 without gaming it, and prove DISPATCH-10 and DISPATCH-11 by mechanism
rather than by argument.
Output: a live priority queue, a reachability test, an extraction diff, and a compiled contract
assertion.

**This plan runs only on the continue branch of the 198-04 checkpoint.**
</objective>
