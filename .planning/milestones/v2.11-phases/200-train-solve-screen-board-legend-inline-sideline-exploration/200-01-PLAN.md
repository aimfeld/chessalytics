<objective>
Turn the Train reveal sidebar into the board's own legend: every line box carries an
arrow glyph in that move's exact board-arrow color, and hovering (desktop) or tapping
the glyph (mobile) hides every other arrow and badge on the shared board.

This plan LEADS the phase with a production-quality tracer slice — the pure spotlight
filter, the glyph icon, one hover target, and the board actually responding — verified
end to end before any expansion task. It then restructures the boxes into real
`Card`/`CardHeader` components (D-01) and splits the desktop-hover / mobile-tap
interaction (D-06/D-07/D-08/D-09).

Purpose: LEGEND-01, LEGEND-02, LEGEND-05, LEGEND-06 — make the overlapping arrow stack
readable without a second board and without leaving the solve screen.
Output: `applyTrainSpotlight` + `trainGlyphColor` in the pure builder, `ArrowGlyphIcon`,
`useIsDesktop`, and a Card-based reveal sidebar wired to the shared board.
