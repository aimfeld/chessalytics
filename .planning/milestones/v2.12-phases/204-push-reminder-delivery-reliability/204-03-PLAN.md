<objective>
Close the VAPID rotation story: the gesture path stops reusing a subscription minted under a
different key, and the rotation procedure stops living only in an archived planning file.

Purpose: `ensureDeviceSubscribed`'s `existing ??` (`push.ts:143-144`) is the D4 defect — it
reuses whatever `getSubscription()` returns without ever comparing the key, which means that
after a rotation even toggling reminders off and on reuses the same dead subscription forever.
That is not a rotation nicety; it is the loss of the ONLY recovery Phase 201's D-02 ever
promised. Plan 01 built the comparison and wired it into the passive path as a suppressor;
this plan wires it into the gesture path as a repair.

Output: the reuse-vs-repair branch in `ensureDeviceSubscribed` with its tests, a runbook at
`docs/push-vapid-rotation-runbook.md` reachable from both the send path and the repo's
operational map, and a real-device verification of the phase's two manual-only behaviors.

**Criterion 4's narrowing, restated here because this is the plan that owns it.** ROADMAP
success criterion 4 reads "a VAPID public-key change causes devices to detect the mismatch and
re-subscribe rather than silently hold a dead subscription; the rotation procedure is written
down." CONTEXT.md D-04 narrows the first half to "…on the user's next reminder-related
gesture" — the score-screen button, the Settings toggle, or the resurface banner, which are
the three and only callers of `ensureDeviceSubscribed`. The passive app-load path from Plan 01
detects a mismatch and deliberately does nothing (D-05). The reasoning: Phase 201 D-02 is
LOCKED as "rotation is accepted mass invalidation, rotate only on key compromise, and truncate
`push_subscriptions` when you do", and that decision explicitly REJECTED a public-key-mismatch
self-heal as real machinery for an event that may never happen. Re-adding it on a passive path
would re-open a settled decision on a hypothetical. The verifier should evaluate the narrowed
criterion. The escape hatch is recorded in CONTEXT.md D-04 and repeated in the runbook: a
passive repair guarded on `Notification.permission === 'granted'` provably cannot spend the
one-shot permission, so reversing this costs no re-research — it is declined on D-02 grounds,
not on PERM-01 grounds. Do NOT satisfy the unnarrowed wording by adding a passive-path
`subscribe()`.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
