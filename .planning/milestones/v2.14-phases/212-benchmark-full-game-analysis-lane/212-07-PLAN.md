The rate implied by those figures — 76,040 games in 203 seconds, about 374 games per second —
is far above what remote Stockfish evaluation can produce. It points at the in-process
`_eval_drain_tick` path, which claims ten games per tick through `_pick_pending_game_ids` and
stamps them complete when the batch yields no eval targets (the documented zero-target
invariant in `app/services/eval_entry.py`). `212-VERIFICATION.md` attributes the leak to the
`/entry-lease` endpoint. Both consumers route through the same canonical claim, which is why
the fix belongs at that claim and not only at the router.
