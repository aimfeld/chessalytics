<objective>
Give the analysis board the same download visibility bot play got, and fix the
signal that currently makes its FlawChess skeleton lie.

Two things are being repaired here, not one. `engineLoading`
(`Analysis.tsx:1090`) is already honest — it is backed by `useStockfishEngine`'s
real `readyok` handshake. `flawChessLoading` (`:1094`) is not: `useFlawChessEngine`

`createWorkerPool()`/`createMaiaQueue()`, before any worker has spawned or any
byte has moved, so its skeleton vanishes almost immediately regardless of
download state. D-12 cannot be honest without fixing that.

There is no gate here — the analysis board has no clock, so progress is
informational and the board stays fully interactive throughout.

Purpose: a first-time visitor on /analysis sees why the engine cards are empty.
Output: honest `useFlawChessEngine` readiness, a progress-aware
`EngineLinesSkeleton`, and all three skeleton slots plus the Maia chart wired.
</objective>
