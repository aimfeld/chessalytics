# Phase 190.1 Plan 03: Reveal Panel Rebuild + Answer-Key Slimming Summary

**Rebuilt the Train reveal panel into three coincidence-merged, client-engine-sourced line boxes with answer-stating verdict rows and an opponent-and-date footer, then retired `best_move`/`best_move_san`/`pv` from the reveal API end to end in one commit.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~55 min
- **Completed:** 2026-07-26
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 11 (1 new frontend lib file, 5 frontend source/test, 3 backend source, 1 backend test, plus the pre-existing files touched across all three tasks)

## Accomplishments

- `TrainReveal`'s verdict rows now spell out the committed guess (`GUESS_LABELS`, shared with `TrainSolveScreen`'s guess buttons) and the played move's SAN (derived client-side via chess.js from `playedMoveUci` + `puzzle.fen`) alongside the server-computed marks/points — never bare `✓`/`✗`.
- The single stored "Best line" block is replaced by up to three steppable line boxes (your move / best move / played in game), each sourced from the client grading engine (`GradeResult.bestLine`/`.playedLine` from Plan 02, plus the Plan 01 reveal-time game-move search) via a pure `buildLineBoxes()` helper that merges boxes whose first move coincides — testid precedence `your > best > game`, line-data precedence `best > your > game`. When the game move coincides with the played or best move, the reveal-time search is skipped entirely (verified via a `startGameMoveSearch` call-count assertion).
- The D-11 "In the game you played X. Best was Y." miss sentence is retired; the herring "handled well" sentence and the D-12 comeback hint are untouched.
- The embedded `GameCard` is replaced by a compact `train-reveal-footer` (opponent derived from `user_color`, date via the shared `formatDateWithYear`) and a one-line Analyze/Next row; Analyze now deep-links to `puzzle.ply - 1` (the puzzle position itself) instead of `puzzle.ply` (one half-move past it).
- `PuzzleRevealResponse`/`RevealedPuzzle` drop `best_move`/`best_move_san`/`pv` end to end (schema, dataclass, router construction, TS mirror, every test fixture) in one commit; a new exact-key-set backend test is the standing guard against a field creeping back in.
