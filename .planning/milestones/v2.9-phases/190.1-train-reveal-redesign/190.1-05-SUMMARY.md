# Plan 190.1-05 Summary: Operator UAT of the redesigned reveal

## What Was Done

**Task 1 (full pre-merge gate)** ran green with zero drift from the wave-4 run and is
recorded in `7c2d1bbd`. It was re-run in full at ship time before the phase was integrated.

**Task 2 (blocking human-verify checkpoint)** was executed as a live UAT loop against the
real dev backend and dev database, at desktop width and at phone width, covering sharp,
soft/herring, played-the-best-move and clear-blunder puzzles. Rather than one sequential
item-by-item pass, it ran as **nine numbered fix rounds**: the operator worked real
sessions, reported findings, each finding was fixed and re-verified live, and the loop
ended when a round produced no new findings.

The round trail, oldest first:

|---|---|---|
| 1 | `7550424e` | Stepper reset, move prompt, date, quality arrows |
| 2 | `b1376a42` | Line-box quality icons, stepping overlay, Solution button |

a live session: the Analyze → back reveal restore (round 5) and a backend guard so a
completed session can no longer unlock a same-day recompose (`f4d62fa4`).

## Deviations from Plan

The checkpoint's shape deviated: the plan describes one operator pass producing either a
clean discharge or a written gap list. In practice it became a nine-round fix-and-re-verify
loop, so the "gap list" was consumed as it was produced rather than written down at the end.
The plan's `<resume-signal>` contract ("approved", or describe each issue) was honored one
round at a time.

Scope expanded beyond the reveal panel in three places, all operator-requested and all
listed under `key-decisions`: reveal/stepping sounds with a shared mute toggle, the points
flash and Beta badge, and the removal of Phase 190's opt-in tactic-line stepper.

None.

## Issues Encountered

The round-9 eval inconsistency was the only defect that required a design change rather than
a fix: the best-move eval came from the MultiPV-4 mount search (budget split four ways) while
the played move got a full-budget width-1 search one ply deeper, so centipawn readings in
decided positions could disagree across the two searches. Grading the played move from its
matching mount-search rank makes the three boxes consistent by construction and removes a
search from the common path.

table above, including the explicit recording caveat. T-190.1-19 holds: UAT ran on the
operator's own account against the local dev database; no production data or credentials are
recorded here.
