  stays `unresolved`. Manual read: the ambiguity is what "non-gem" means operationally, because
  `best_move_tier_sql` returns NULL both when the best-vs-second gap is small (a genuinely
  several-good-moves position, which is what a herring must be) and when the move was easy to find
  (high `maia_prob` with a large gap, which is a bad herring — the "several fine moves" guess would

  the requirement text alone does not settle it; confirm at verification that the resulting herring
  pool is large enough on real data.
