<objective>
Turn the tracer's single-path composition into the real POOL-07 session: exactly N puzzles at a 75/25
mix, padded honestly, frozen at start, with a lifecycle that resumes, expires and evicts correctly.

Purpose: this is the endpoint Phase 190's whole solve loop is built on. Getting the mix, the
backfill behavior and the open-session semantics right here is what makes "4 of 12" stable and
prevents a backfill-lagging pool from reading as "you are caught up".

Output: `compose_slots` in `train_pool.py`; full composition plus lifecycle in
`train_repository.py`; the widened `TrainSessionResponse`; repository and router tests.
</objective>

Add `compose_slots(n: int) -> tuple[int, int]` to `app/services/train_pool.py`: returns
`(sr_slots, herring_slots)` where `herring_slots = math.floor(n * HERRING_SHARE)` and

remainder because learnable material outranks filler, and that the two always sum to exactly `n`.
Pure function, no I/O.

Widen `compose_and_materialize_session` in `app/repositories/train_repository.py` to the full
POOL-07 algorithm, keeping every await sequential (never `asyncio.gather` on one `AsyncSession`):

1. Resolve settings via `get_or_create_settings`; `n = settings.puzzles_per_session`;
   `today = local_today(settings.timezone, now_utc)`; `sr_slots, herring_slots = compose_slots(n)`.
2. SR candidates: due `drill_items` (status ACTIVE, `due_date <= today`) LEFT-joined to the flaw row
   on `(user_id, game_id, ply)` and INNER-joined to `games`, filtered to rows whose flaw still
   exists (lazy eviction), ordered `due_date ASC, game_id ASC, ply ASC`, limited to `sr_slots`.
3. If fewer than `sr_slots`, pad from `pool_entry_stmt(user_id)` excluding `(game_id, ply)` pairs
   already present in `drill_items` for this user, ordered `Game.played_at DESC NULLS LAST,
   game_id DESC, ply ASC`, and insert a `DrillItem` per pick with `status=ACTIVE, streak=0,
   fail_count=0, ever_correct=False, due_date=today`.
4. Herring candidates: `herring_stmt(user_id, exclude_served=True)` limited to `herring_slots`; when
   that yields nothing, retry once with `exclude_served=False` (source exhausted, repeats allowed).
5. Cross-backfill: if the SR side came up short, take up to `n - len(picked)` extra herrings; if the
   herring side came up short, take up to `n - len(picked)` extra SR candidates (continuing the same
   ordering, without re-selecting anything already picked). Cap the total at `n`.
6. Build each puzzle's `fen` with `full_fen_at_ply(game.pgn, ply)` and `side_to_move` from
   `mover_color_for_ply(ply)`; drop and do not backfill a puzzle whose FEN will not reconstruct.
7. If the final list is empty, return a response with `puzzle_count=0` and an empty `puzzles` list
   and write NO `drill_sessions` row.
8. Otherwise shuffle the final list with `random.Random(f"{user_id}:{today.isoformat()}")` so a
   red herring's position is not inferable from ordering, then insert the `DrillSession`
   (`status='open'`, `session_date=today`, `expires_on=session_window(today, weekday_mask)`,
   `puzzle_count=len(final)`, `started_at=now_utc`) and one `DrillSolve` per puzzle with ascending
   `position`, the correct `DrillSource`, and `solved_at=None`.
9. Compute `blob_pending_count`: the number of the user's own blunder flaws that pass ply parity and
   the winnability floor but whose `missed_pv_lines` is NULL — the count of items still waiting on
   opportunistic tier-4 analysis.

| client → `POST /api/train/sessions` | No body input, but the response shape is the entire pre-attempt surface |
