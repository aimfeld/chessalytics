<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 1: Full-session UAT and backstop discharge</name>
  <precondition>The dev backend and frontend are runnable and the dev database holds at least one account with analyzed games that produce a non-empty drill pool — Train composes only from the user's own qualifying blunders, so an account with no analyzed games can exercise the empty state but not the solve loop.</precondition>
  <what-built>
The complete Train surface: a `/train` tab on the desktop header, the mobile bottom bar, and the More drawer (import-gated like Openings and Endgames, with a first-visit dot chained after Endgames); a landing screen covering fresh, short, resume, completed, and empty sessions; the guess-then-one-move solve loop with client-side WASM grading, a session progress indicator, and the opponent's last move highlighted; an auto-opening reveal with both verdicts, points, neutral-factual copy, a comeback hint, a steppable best line, an opt-in tactic stepper on tagged flaws, the game card, and the analysis deep link; and a session-end score screen with a green, yellow, or red rating.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
Run the dev stack: `docker compose -f docker-compose.dev.yml -p flawchess-dev up -d`, then the backend and `cd frontend && npm run dev`. Sign in as an account with analyzed games.

**A — Nav and gating (NAV-01, NAV-02).**
1. With that account, confirm Train sits second in the desktop header, between Library and Bots, and that clicking it opens `/train`.
2. Open the browser's device toolbar at **320px wide**. Confirm the bottom bar shows six tabs plus More, every label fully readable, nothing truncated, wrapped, or overflowing horizontally, and the tab widths look even. Repeat at 360px and 390px.
3. Open the More drawer and confirm the Train entry is present and correctly placed.
4. Sign in as (or simulate) an account with zero imported games. Confirm Train is greyed on all three surfaces, shows the import-required tooltip, and does not navigate on click — while Library and Bots stay reachable.
5. If you have never visited Train on this account and have already cleared the Openings and Endgames dots, confirm the Train dot shows; visit Train and confirm it does not come back.

**B — Landing states (D-01 to D-04, D-14).**
6. Reload `/train` and watch the initial load: confirm it shows the muted text-only loading treatment, matching how the other routes load — not a new skeleton.
7. Confirm the landing does not auto-start: a status line and one button, nothing else.
8. Solve one puzzle, navigate away mid-session, and return: confirm the button now reads as a Resume with the solved and total counts, and that pressing it lands on the next unsolved puzzle rather than the first.
9. If the account's pool yields a one-puzzle session, read every count string on the landing and in the loop and confirm they read grammatically at one.

**C — Solve loop (SOLV-01 to SOLV-04).**
10. On a fresh puzzle, try to drag a piece before guessing: nothing should move. At **320px**, confirm the two guess controls wrap onto two lines rather than truncating.
11. Commit a guess, then play a move. Confirm the board orientation matches your colour, the opponent's last move is highlighted, and there is no eval bar, clock, player name, or game metadata anywhere.
12. Confirm the progress indicator and its bar are visible throughout, on both the desktop width and at 390px.
13. Play a clearly bad move on one puzzle and watch the checking indicator: it should appear in place of the prompt with the played move still on the board, no overlay and no board flicker, and resolve within a couple of seconds.
14. On another puzzle, play what you believe is the best move; if it matches the engine's, the verdict should appear with no checking wait at all.

**D — Forced failures.**
15. Open devtools and block the request to `/api/train/sessions/*/solve` (or go offline), then solve a puzzle. Confirm the failure copy and a Retry button appear, that Next is disabled and does not advance, and that unblocking and pressing Retry recovers and re-enables Next.
16. Block the request for `/engine/stockfish-18-lite-single.js` (or `.wasm`) and reload into a session. Confirm you are not left on the checking indicator: an inline engine error with a retry affordance should appear.

**E — Reveal and score (SOLV-05 to SOLV-07).**
17. On a missed puzzle, confirm the reveal opens by itself, the board snaps back to the puzzle position, and both verdict rows plus the points tally are shown. Confirm the panel sits beside the board on desktop and below it at 390px.
18. Step through the best line with the prev and next controls, and confirm the ends disable.
19. Find a tactic-tagged puzzle: confirm the step-through control is offered, is labelled with the motif name, and that nothing tactic-related loads or renders until you press it.
20. Confirm the game card renders and its Analyze link opens the analysis board at the right game and move.
21. At 390px, look for a long motif name or a deep line and confirm nothing breaks the reveal card layout.
22. Finish the session and confirm the score screen shows the total out of twice the session size, a percentage, and a coloured rating.

**F — Copy read (the three flagged prohibitions).**
23. Read every string you saw in the loop — the miss sentence, the herring sentence, the comeback hint, the verdict rows, and the red or yellow rating — and answer three questions: does any of it shame, blame, or characterise you as a player rather than stating what happened? Does the rating read as a judgement of your chess ability rather than of this one session's answers? Does any copy claim a move is objectively or definitively the only correct one, when the grading is a bounded local search?

Report anything that fails with the step number, the viewport, and what you saw.
