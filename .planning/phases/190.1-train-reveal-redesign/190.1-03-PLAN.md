<assumption_delta_decision>
**Noun now primary:** client-engine-computed answer data (`GradeResult.bestLine`,
`GradeResult.playedLine`, the reveal-time game-move line) — not the server-stored answer
key (`PuzzleRevealResponse.best_move` / `best_move_san` / `pv`).

**Decision: `promote`.** The stored answer-key line fields are removed from the reveal
response in this plan, not kept as a fallback.

**Rationale:** D-01 exists precisely because stored and client evals are not reproducible
across machines, so a "fallback" that silently substitutes stored numbers would reintroduce
the exact contradiction (a YOUR MOVE eval disagreeing with the verdict) this phase was
raised to remove. A field that may never be displayed is not a fallback — it is an unused
field behind a hand-mirrored type with no codegen to keep it honest. Removing it also
shrinks the answer-adjacent payload behind the 409 gate, which is a net security
improvement.

**What is kept:** `played_in_game_san` stays. It is the plain historical fact of which move
was played (straight from `game_positions.move_san`), not an engine-derived line or eval, and
it is the honest label for the played-in-game box when the reveal-time engine search fails.
`puzzle_type`, `has_tactic_lines`, `source`, `game_id`, `ply`, `fen` all stay unchanged.

**Superseded copy (flagged, not silently dropped):** the Phase 190 D-11 miss sentence
("In the game you played X. Best was Y.") is removed. Its two facts are now stated with
evals by the played-in-game and best-move boxes, and its `best_move_san` data source is
one of the fields being removed. The D-11 herring sentence and the D-12 comeback hint are
NOT removed — SOLV-05 requires the herring framing, and neither depends on a removed field.
</assumption_delta_decision>
