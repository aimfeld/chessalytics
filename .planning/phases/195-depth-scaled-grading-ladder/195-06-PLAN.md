<objective>
Close the loop: measure the three candidate ladders as ladders, let the committed rule pick one,
ship its rungs, and report the end-to-end result at both budgets.

Everything before this plan deliberately avoided choosing rungs. Plan 01 shipped a provisional
flat table so the plumbing changed delivered depth by zero; Plan 05 measured flat depths and
derived candidates without touching the module. This plan is where the numbers become the engine.

The rung commit is costly to reverse — Phase 199 runs one combined recalibration sweep against
whatever ships here, so changing the rungs afterwards invalidates the calibration measured
through them. That is why the operator approved the frontier at Plan 05's checkpoint before this
plan starts.

Purpose: LADDER-01's "the data selects the rungs", LADDER-02's observed variation, and
LADDER-05's end-to-end report.
Output: the shipped ladder, its confirming measurements, and the phase report.
</objective>
