<objective>
Feed the free MultiPV=2 Stockfish run's settled moves into the FlawChess root exactly once per
position on genuine disagreement, and make the practical score for Stockfish's pick actually reach the
existing verdict row.

Purpose: Plan 196-01 made the search core honour an injected root move. This plan supplies the moves
and closes the display gap. Two non-obvious hazards dominate: an unstable `extraRootMoves` identity
would restart-storm the search during Stockfish's ~1.5-2 s pre-commit window, and the prop currently
wired into the verdict's lookup is truncated to the top 2 findability-ranked lines — so a genuinely
strong-but-unfindable injected move would be legitimately demoted out of the lookup and the practical
line would still never render.

Output: an `extraRootMoves` option on `useFlawChessEngine`; a stable-identity, latched-per-position
disagreement supply in `Analysis.tsx`; an additive unsliced `RankedLine[]` memo for the verdict's
lookup; and the tests that make "exactly once" and "the line populates" real rather than asserted.
</objective>
