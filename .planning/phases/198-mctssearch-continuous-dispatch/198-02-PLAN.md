   expansion; perfect overlap costs `max(P, G/c)`; so the modelled reduction is
   `1 − max(P, G/c) / (P + G/c)`, capped at 50 percent and reached only when `P` equals `G/c`.

   a modelled reduction of 25 percent or more means build it; strictly below 25 and at or above
   15 percent means raise a checkpoint out loud and the operator decides; strictly below 15
   percent means exit the phase. State explicitly which inequality is inclusive at each boundary
   and that no rounding is applied to the computed percentage before comparison. State what
   happens when the two budgets land in different bands: the phase takes the LOWER band, because
   the bot budget is the one the shipped bot actually runs, and a checkpoint is raised naming both.
   State that these bands were written knowing U-04's algebra pencils out near 19 percent — which
   is the point.

5. `## 4. Why the grading-ladder rule is not reused` — briefly: that rule's

   because the old one is wrong.
