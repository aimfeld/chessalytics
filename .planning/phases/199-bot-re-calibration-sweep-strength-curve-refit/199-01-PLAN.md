- **P-04 — derived timing baseline only (A-02).** Parse the local pre-195 logs and commit a KB-scale
  derived artifact; never `git add -f` the ~17.5 MB of raw `run.log`. See plan 05.
- **P-05 — the new sweep out-dirs are gitignored (NOT flagged by research).**
  `.gitignore:237-238` excludes `reports/data/sweep-*/` and `reports/data/persona-sweep-*/`, and
