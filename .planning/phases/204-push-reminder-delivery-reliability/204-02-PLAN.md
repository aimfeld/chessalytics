<objective>
Two backend fixes that share the same five lines of `_process_candidate` and therefore ship
together: a push message stops being discarded the instant the phone is unreachable (D5), and
a fan-out that delivered to nobody stops burning the whole day's reminder claim (D3).

Purpose: D5 is what makes "sent" mean anything — while `TTL: 0` stands, a discarded message
and a real delivery are byte-identical server-side (both 201, both increment `sent`), which
is why CONTEXT.md D-16 gates every future push metric behind this fix. D3 is what makes Plan
01's re-sync pay off the SAME day: prune at 16:05 → the user opens the app at 18:00 → Plan
01's re-sync restores the row → the 18:15 tick sends, but only if this plan released the
claim. Without it, Plan 01's repair produces nothing until tomorrow.

Output: a timezone-aware TTL helper beside `local_today`/`local_hour`, a keyword-only
`ttl_seconds` threaded through both `push_send` entry points, a guarded `release_reminder_claim`
repository function, a new step 8 in `_process_candidate`, the tests that pin all of it, and
a durable decision log for D3.

**These two changes touch the same region of `_process_candidate`** (the `send_to_user` call
and the lines around it), which is why they are one plan rather than two parallel ones — two
plans would collide on the same file and force a wasted wave. They share no files at all with
Plan 01, so this plan runs in wave 1 alongside it.
</objective>
