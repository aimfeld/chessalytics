<objective>
Deliver the THIN slice of SEED-146 item 4 (D-03): a global concurrency cap on import
execution, a visible bare "queued" job state, and the reaper exemption that keeps a
queued-but-alive job from being marked failed.

Purpose: past roughly 20 concurrent imports in the flush phase, requests from users who
are not importing hang for 30 s on the connection pool and then 500. A global cap converts
that site-wide failure into a bounded wait with an honest label.
Output: one module-level semaphore, one new in-memory job status, one `started_at`
re-stamp at slot acquisition, three active-job call sites widened, the wire type tightened,
and a bare queued label on the import page — each proven by revert-red.
</objective>
