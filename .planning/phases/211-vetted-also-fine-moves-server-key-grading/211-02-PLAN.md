# Plan 211-02: Width 1 — retire the client's own "also fine" derivation

<objective>
Take the client engine out of the business of proposing alternatives, and give its whole
budget to the one line it is still authoritative for.

Purpose: Plan 01 made the server the source of the alternative set. Until this plan lands,
the client still computes a competing one from a budget-split MultiPV-4 search — two lists,
one board. This retires the client's list, drops the mount search to width 1 (D-05), and
splits the arrow cap that was silently derived from that width (RESEARCH Pitfall 6).

Output: `TRAIN_GRADING_MULTIPV_WIDTH` at 1; the client-side fine-move derivation, its
grading-result field and the mount-rank grading shortcut all gone; three independent
per-puzzle-type arrow caps; the D-04 residual documented where the grading rule lives.
</objective>
