<objective>
Produce the end-to-end pipeline proof BENCHLANE-04 has been waiting for, on the 20-game smoke
tranche and nothing wider.

Purpose: this plan is the tracer slice for the tranche. 212-06 committed the fleet to a run
that aborted in 80 seconds on a defect nobody had seen, and the phase has still never observed
a single game go through the real pipeline against the benchmark database. Proving one game end
to end on the gated build costs minutes; discovering the same class of defect after committing
3.4 days of fleet time costs days. Every task-level slice here is measurement or operator
action — the pipeline itself already exists and is unchanged, so no `type="tracer"` task is
emitted; the plan as a whole is the thin slice, and 212-09 and 212-10 are the expansion.

Output: a durable smoke record carrying the before and after numbers, and a go or no-go
decision for committing fleet time to the real tranche.
</objective>
