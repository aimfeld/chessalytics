## Claude's Discretion, resolved

CONTEXT.md leaves open whether to add a re-vendor helper script or to do the copy by hand with a
documented audit trail. **Resolved toward the documented command**, the lighter option: the six
files are copied with one `cp` invocation that is recorded verbatim in
`frontend/public/maia/README.md`, and provenance is proven by SHA-256 equality against
`node_modules/`, which is stronger evidence than a script's existence. A committed helper would
add a maintained artifact plus a `knip` surface for a procedure that runs once per bump. The
`wasmBinary` suppression check is likewise a throwaway Node script in the scratchpad, matching the
Phase 213-09 precedent, with its result recorded in the README and the SUMMARY.
