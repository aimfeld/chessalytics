<objective>
Implement SEED-141: exclude Train drill candidates whose second-best move still leaves
the mover clearly winning (>= +200 centipawns mover POV, or outright mating for the mover).

Purpose: a drill puzzle is only fair when failing to find the move actually costs
something. Today `pool_entry_stmt` admits a blunder on the strength of the best-vs-second
GAP (`dead_band_admissible`) plus a winnability floor on the position before the move.
Neither notices "you were +6, best is +9, runner-up is +4" — a puzzle asking the user to
distinguish two winning moves. Measured on prod (2026-08-08, 795,267 candidates /
266 users): the +2 rule removes 23.9% of the pool, 90.7% of the cut is soft material, and
the sharp share rises 31.9% -> 38.9%. Starvation is negligible (median distinct games
462 -> 403; 2 users drop to zero, both already effectively empty and covered by the
herring / sharp-filler cross-backfill).

The operator has RESOLVED the seed's one open question: **+2, not +3.**

Output: one named centipawn constant plus one sibling SQL predicate in
`app/services/train_pool.py`, applied at all three SR read sites, with unit tests that
would fail on a sign bug, a mate-sign bug, or the NULL-under-NOT bug; plus a CHANGELOG
entry.
</objective>
