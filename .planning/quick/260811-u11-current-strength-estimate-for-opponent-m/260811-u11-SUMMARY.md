# Quick Task 260811-u11: Current-strength estimate for opponent matching Summary

**90-day/20-game-median current-strength estimate, recency-ranked across (platform, TC) rungs with a native-Lichess-blitz tiebreak, replacing the 36-month career-median anchor on all three opponent-matching surfaces (Bots page, custom-bot setup, free-play ELO default).**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~35 min
- **Tasks:** 3 (backend estimator/schema/router; frontend repoint; pre-merge gate + changelog + seed close)
- **Files modified:** 21 (6 created, 15 modified)

## Accomplishments

- New `current_strength_repository.fetch_recent_rungs` reads qualifying games exclusively through `apply_game_filters(rated=True, opponent_type="human", platform=None)` — the regression-proof test seeds 5 populations (rated human lichess blitz, FlawChess bot games, unrated human games, rated-vs-computer games, pasted PGNs) and asserts exactly one rung survives, at exactly the signal rating.
- New `current_strength_service.resolve_current_strength` implements the full D-01→D-03/D-07/P-02 selection policy as a pure function: 90-day/20-game activity floor, 20-game median normalized via `normalize_to_lichess_blitz`, recency ranking across every surviving rung, a native-Lichess-blitz tiebreak (≤7 days lag, no point-gap condition — revised post-execution against real prod data), and a blitz-bucket-anchor fallback when nothing qualifies.
- `UserProfileResponse.current_strength` replaces `lichess_blitz_equivalent_rating` on the wire entirely (P-01) — `GET`/`PUT /users/me/profile` now take a `dev_now_utc` dependency and reuse the already-fetched anchors dict rather than re-querying.
- All three opponent-matching surfaces repointed: `PersonaGrid`'s rating line, `Bots.tsx`'s `SetupScreen.normalizedRating` prop, and `useMaiaEloDefault`'s free-play branch — plus a new `currentStrengthCopy` helper that names the rung (platform, TC, game count, window, whether converted) in the Bots-page popover.
- Nothing in `frontend/src` or `app/` references the deleted `lichess_blitz_equivalent_rating` field any more (verified by grep after each edit round).
