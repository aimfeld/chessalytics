What real games reveal about how humans actually play chess

Interactive analyses built on the FlawChess benchmark database: hundreds of thousands of Lichess games with full Stockfish evaluations, across every rating level and time control.

August 15, 2026

You're two pawns up. Do you actually win?

460,604 games say a "winning position" is a promise you still have to keep: players who reach the middlegame at a Stockfish evaluation of +2.0 or better lose roughly one game in four, and a bare two-pawn lead goes down almost a third of the time. Plus the two ways a won game is lost: outplayed, or flagged.
